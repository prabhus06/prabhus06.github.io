[ About Me ]

Executive Profile

I'm a Strategic QA Test Manager with over 17 years of experience turning quality into a competitive advantage, not an afterthought. My path started as a hands-on engineer and grew into leading enterprise scale QA programmes across retail and telecoms, where I've built and led distributed, multi vendor teams of 50+ engineers spanning multiple time zones.

I believe great quality engineering is as much about culture as it is about code. I focus on building high performing teams through mentoring, strong vendor governance, and a quality first mindset that gets embedded early, not bolted on at the end. That approach has delivered real business impact including £324K+ in annual savings

My expertise spans the full quality lifecycle Functional, API, UI, Microservices, Performance, Accessibility, and Release Governance with a growing focus on scalable test automation and AI augmented testing. I'm driven by the challenge of building systems and teams that don't just catch issues, but prevent them turning quality into something users never have to think about, because it just works.
